Transit fares frozen until 2027
By Lena Okoye | 3 Feb 2026
The regional transit authority approved a fare freeze on Monday, keeping bus and tram prices flat until 2027.
Officials said higher ridership since the spring had covered rising fuel costs, making an increase unnecessary.
Read more: How the tram extension is going
Commuter groups welcomed the decision but asked for more frequent service on the night routes.
The authority will review the night network in January, with changes expected by the summer timetable.
