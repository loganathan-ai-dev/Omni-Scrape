Building Cedar Raised Beds

Posted on May 2, 2025 by Helen Ruiz

Last spring I finally replaced the rotten raised beds behind the garage with cedar boxes.

Cedar costs more than pine, but it resists rot without chemical treatment, which matters for vegetables.

I built three beds, each four feet by eight feet, and filled them with a mix of compost and topsoil.

The first season produced more tomatoes than we could eat, so we started canning in August.

If you build your own, line the bottom with hardware cloth to keep the voles out.
